# Community conduct

CapEgo welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Be respectful, explain technical disagreements with evidence, and give actionable feedback. Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, or publish another person's private information. Keep discussions relevant to the project and acknowledge the source of others' work.

Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive contributions and restrict participation when needed. For a concern requiring private discussion, ask a repository maintainer for a private contact channel without posting sensitive details publicly.
